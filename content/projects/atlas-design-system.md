## Overview

Atlas Design System is a placeholder case study about reducing interface drift across a small internal product family.

## Approach

The work starts with a small set of shared primitives, naming conventions, and interaction states. The goal is consistency without turning the system into a heavy governance layer.

## Outcome

The placeholder result is a practical foundation that makes common product surfaces faster to design and implement.
