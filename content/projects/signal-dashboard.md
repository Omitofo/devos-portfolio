## Overview

Signal Dashboard is a placeholder operations view for a small team that needs to understand what requires attention at a glance.

## Approach

The information hierarchy separates immediate exceptions from routine status. Compact summaries support scanning while detailed context remains available on demand.

## Outcome

The placeholder interface gives a team a calmer operational overview and a clearer path from signal to action.
