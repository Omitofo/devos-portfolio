## Overview

Cascade CLI is a placeholder developer-tool concept for turning structured content into predictable static output.

## Approach

The workflow treats content as source code: files live in the repository, transformations are explicit, and the generated site remains easy to inspect.

## Outcome

The placeholder tool demonstrates a simple publishing path that avoids a database or complex editorial interface.
